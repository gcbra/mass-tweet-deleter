# Privacy policy

Mass Tweet Deleter does not collect, store or share any of your data.

- Everything runs in your own browser, in your own x.com tab.
- The only network requests it makes go to x.com, to delete your posts, using the session you are already signed in with.
- Your archive file, if you use one, is read in your browser and never uploaded anywhere.
- There are no analytics, no tracking and no servers run by this project.
- It remembers one thing: whether the panel is open in the current tab (in that tab's session storage), so it can reappear after a page reload. Closing the panel or the tab clears it.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/gcbra/mass-tweet-deleter/issues.
